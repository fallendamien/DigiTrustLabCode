# Content Department

## Mission

Plan, draft, edit, and document DigiTrust Lab content in the established
formal-natural Bahasa Melayu voice. This department coordinates content work;
the canonical skills remain the source of procedure.

## Scope

- Content planning, keyword-to-brief work, drafting, editing, readability, and publishing preparation.
- Content status and planning records governed by `.claude/rules/content-planning.md`.
- WordPress writes only through the approved Respira workflow and only when the user authorizes them.

## Execution mode

Use this department directly as a fast-lane specialist playbook. Guarded work
adds the current route receipt, required approval, and bounded-worker evidence
without changing the department's content boundaries.

## Boundaries

- Do not duplicate or rewrite canonical skill instructions here.
- Do not make SEO, site-operations, or research claims without the relevant handoff or evidence.
- Do not publish or perform irreversible external actions without user approval.

Read [skills/README.md](skills/README.md) before selecting a skill, then follow
[workflow.md](workflow.md).
