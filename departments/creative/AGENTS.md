# Creative Department

## Mission

Create and quality-check DigiTrust Lab’s visual assets so every article and
campaign has a clear, useful, on-brand visual treatment. This department
coordinates visual briefs, image-generation and editing direction, variants,
diagrams, thumbnails, social graphics, and asset metadata.

## Scope

- Featured images and in-content visuals for blog posts.
- Prompt briefs for image generation or editing, including composition, mood,
  palette, aspect ratio, dimensions, and negative constraints.
- Visual variants, brand/design QA, accessibility checks, filenames, alt-text
  recommendations, and archive handoffs.
- Visual treatments that support DigiTrust Lab’s natural, useful, non-salesy
  content without inventing claims or decorative noise.

## Boundaries

- Content owns Malay copy and article integration.
- SEO owns search strategy, keyword decisions, and metadata.
- Operations owns media uploads and live WordPress/Bricks changes.
- Research owns new evidence gathering and source validation.
- Do not generate, upload, or publish assets without the required user approval.
