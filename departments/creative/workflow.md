# Creative workflow

## Bounded specialist loop

1. Confirm the content goal, reader, placement, visual format, dimensions,
   aspect ratio, brand/design source of truth, and any existing asset.
2. Select one canonical skill from `skills/README.md` and read it in full.
3. Produce the bounded artifact: visual brief, generation/edit prompt, variant
   set, visual QA report, or asset handoff. Do not silently write article copy.
4. Record the exact filename, format, dimensions, alt-text recommendation,
   provenance, and archive destination for every delivered asset.
5. Stop at the department boundary when the task requires copy, SEO decisions,
   new research, or live uploads; create a handoff instead of improvising.

## DigiTrust Lab visual guardrails

- Follow `DESIGN.md` and the established site palette and typography when the
  asset is for DigiTrust Lab.
- Keep visuals practical, legible, and calm. Avoid hype, income promises,
  unrequested logos, fake UI, fabricated screenshots, and text that the image
  model may render inaccurately.
- Prefer a clean visual hierarchy with enough contrast and whitespace for blog
  thumbnails and mobile readers. Treat text in generated images as optional;
  supply important wording through Content or the page itself.
- Alt text describes the useful visual content, not keyword stuffing.

## Verification gate

Before handoff or completion, verify dimensions, aspect ratio, format,
filename, visual consistency, contrast/readability, unwanted artifacts, and
the requested variant count. Confirm provenance and archive handoff. A prompt
or local plan is not evidence that an image was generated, copied, uploaded,
or live on WordPress.

## Codex handoff

Provide: objective, source files, brief or asset produced, prompt/tool
provenance, dimensions and formats, filename/archive details, QA evidence,
open judgment calls, and the exact next action requested from Content, SEO,
Operations, or Research.
