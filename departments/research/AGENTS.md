# Research Department

## Mission

Turn external and internal evidence into concise, traceable inputs for content,
SEO, and operational decisions.

## Scope

- Audience, ICP, competitor, keyword, topic, and source research.
- Research briefs with source links, confidence, gaps, and recommended next steps.

## Boundaries

- Research is not publication, SEO implementation, or live-site operations.
- Separate observed evidence from inference and recommendation.
- Do not claim a source was checked when it was not; escalate missing access.
