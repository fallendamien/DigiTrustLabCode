## Overview

The Plagiarism Checker verifies the uniqueness of your content by checking for duplicate content on the web. It is a supporting tool designed to help you fine-tune your content before publishing.

## Popular Use Cases

- Verifying the integrity of your content (articles, blogs, papers, etc.)
- Making sure the content you create is original enough
- Avoiding repeated text that negatively affects user experience and your Time on Page metric

## How It Works

The Plagiarism Checker compares your content against a large database of information and online data banks to determine how much of it is similar to other content.

**Key mechanic:** The tool checks only against content that is **indexed on Google** — not Google Scholar or other internet libraries.

> **Why indexed content only?** Duplicating content that already appears in the SERPs is what directly impacts your rankings. Non-indexed content (e.g. PDFs not indexed as URLs) does not affect your SEO, so it is intentionally excluded from the check.
